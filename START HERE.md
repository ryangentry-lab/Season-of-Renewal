**Purpose** - Starting point for planning a Season of Renewal. The steps within this framework are meant to help outline various pillars that will ultimately support a major life transition. 

The [[README]] is primarily for development logging and scope clarification. It is not necessary to read in order to begin.

# Your Season
[[Starting Point]] - This is a more detailed note to launch your planning process. It is recommended you spend some time in each of the numbered folders to intake and digest the contents. As you go along, you'll be writing directly into them!
This is your **before** state. Without an accurate assessment of where you currently are, it'll be hard to measure the future state. It is assumed you are in some level of burn out, so treat this section gently. 

[[Name Your Season]] - This is your desired **after** state. Where do you want to be at the end of this season? 

# Phases
[[Departure]] - This is preparation for the major life transition. Treat this phase like a gift, it is not often we get to choose when a major life transition happens, usually they just happen. By being intentional with this choice, and measured in your execution will you find security in taking the leap.

[[Recovery]] - Intentional decompression. This will be the most difficult phase of your season. 

[[Soul Search]] - Discovery

[[Strengthen]] - Intentional improvement.

[[Action]] - Moving into what's next
